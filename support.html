Help & Support

How can we help?

For account access, privacy, deletion, syncing, safety, or app behavior, open a support request. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, full workout exports, or other sensitive information in a public request.

Create a support request

Account deletion

In Liftmaxing, open Settings and choose Delete Account & Data. This permanently removes the account and associated training profile. If deletion fails, keep the account signed in and create a support request.

Safety

Liftmaxing provides general fitness information, not medical advice. For an emergency, contact local emergency services. For pain, injury, or health concerns, stop training and consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Useful report details

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, sign-in provider, the screen involved, and steps to reproduce the problem. Never post access tokens or private keys.

Support is handled through the Liftmaxing Support GitHub repository.
