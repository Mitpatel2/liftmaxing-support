Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you sign in, Liftmaxing receives a provider account identifier and may receive your name and email address from Apple or Google. We store information you enter or generate through app use, including body weight, completed sets, load, repetitions, RIR, rest time, workout duration, estimated one-rep maxes, templates, training level, and preferences.

How we use information

We use this data for authentication, account sync, workout history, progress charts, personalized load and repetition guidance, data export, support, security, and features you request. Liftmaxing does not receive your Apple or Google password.

Storage and providers

Account and training data is processed by Supabase as our authentication and hosting provider. Apple and Google process information needed for their sign-in services. A local profile copy may be stored on your iPhone. Authentication tokens use iOS Keychain-backed storage and network requests use HTTPS.

Food diary and database search

Food diary entries, portions, calories, and macronutrients stay in app storage on this device, separately for each account. They are not synced to Supabase. They are included when you export your data. Search terms and scanned product barcodes are sent to wger for public food database lookup; that service also receives normal network information such as your IP address. We do not send your account identity or diary to wger. Food records show their source and license attribution, including Open Food Facts where applicable.

Camera and meal photos

Camera access is optional, for barcode scanning and meal photos. You may also choose a photo. Meal photos are processed on your device using Apple Vision and are not uploaded to Supabase or an external AI service. Photos are not attached to saved food diary entries; temporary image files may remain in the operating system or app cache until cleared. Photo labels are suggestions, not measurements of calories or portions.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

With your permission, Liftmaxing reads active and resting energy from Apple Health, including activity synced from Apple Watch, and writes dietary energy for newly confirmed food entries. Health readings are displayed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, used for advertising, or sold. Food syncing is optional. You can turn it off in the Food tab and manage individual permissions in Apple Health. Turning syncing off does not erase previously saved Health entries. Removing a synced food entry queues removal of its corresponding app-owned Health sample; failed removals remain pending until successfully retried.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information, use it for third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. We disclose information only to service providers needed to operate Liftmaxing, when you direct us to, when legally required, or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while your account exists. Settings provides data export and permanent account deletion. Deletion removes the Supabase Auth account and associated cloud profile, then clears local account data and the food diary. When available, stored Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked as part of deletion. Entries already saved to Apple Health remain there after account deletion; manage or delete them in the Health app.

Your choices

You may sign out, export your data, delete your account, or manage provider authorization from Apple or Google settings. Camera and Apple Health permissions can be managed in iOS settings. Food can be entered manually without camera or Health access. To request privacy help, use our support page.

Children

Liftmaxing is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account if you are below the minimum age required in your country.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
