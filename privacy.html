Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you sign in, Liftmaxing receives a provider account identifier and may receive your name and email address from Apple or Google. We store information you enter or generate through app use, including body weight, completed sets, load, repetitions, RIR, rest time, workout duration, estimated one-rep maxes, templates, training level, and preferences.

How we use information

We use this data for authentication, account sync, workout history, progress charts, personalized load and repetition guidance, data export, support, security, and features you request. Liftmaxing does not receive your Apple or Google password.

TestFlight prediction evaluation

TestFlight evaluation builds can learn a small personal rep-prediction model on your device from earlier completed workouts. Predictions and model-version details sync with your existing workout history so we can compare predicted and logged reps. Normal recommendations do not change. The app owner can view aggregate evaluation errors, not other testers' identifiable workout records through this feature. Turn off Prediction evaluation in Settings to stop new predictions. Existing evaluation records follow the same export and account-deletion controls as workout history.

Storage and providers

Account and training data is processed by Supabase as our authentication and hosting provider. Apple and Google process information needed for their sign-in services. A local profile copy may be stored on your iPhone. Authentication tokens use iOS Keychain-backed storage and network requests use HTTPS.

Food diary and database search

Food diary entries, portions, calories, and macronutrients stay in app storage on this device, separately for each account. They are included in data exports. Food search terms and barcodes are sent through our catalog service to Open Food Facts. Our service receives normal network information such as your IP address and caches public product results under hashed search keys. It does not receive your diary or Health readings, or send your account identity to Open Food Facts. Food data is provided by Open Food Facts contributors under the Open Database License.

Camera and meal photos

Camera access is optional, for barcode scanning and meal photos. Recognition runs on-device. Photos stay on your device unless you explicitly select Agree & upload to contribute a photo. Photos are not attached to saved food diary entries; temporary image files may remain in the operating system or app cache until cleared. Photo labels are suggestions, not measurements of calories or portions.

Optional food-photo contributions

With your separate agreement for each contribution, we store a resized photo, your confirmed dish name, account ID and consent time/version privately in Supabase. We remove embedded location metadata. Authorized reviewers may inspect these contributions for quality and use approved examples to improve food recognition. Only share photos you own, without people or personal information. Standalone photo contributions are private and are not sold or used for advertising. You may decline and still use food logging. Delete an upload under Settings → My photo contributions, or delete your account, to remove the uploaded photo and label and prevent future use. Deletion cannot undo learning in a model already released. Contributions are retained until deleted; operational upload counters may remain until account deletion.

Community recipes

With explicit recipe-sharing consent, we store your recipe ingredients, portions, instructions, meal photo and consent version. After content review, other signed-in users can view and log the recipe with an unverified-nutrition label. Your email and account identifier are not displayed. The photo and confirmed dish label also enter the quality-review pipeline for future food-recognition training; uploading does not automatically improve the model. Delete the submission in Settings → My photo contributions to remove its shared recipe and photo and stop future training use. Copies already logged by others may remain, and deletion cannot undo learning in a released model. Users can report recipes; moderators can unpublish them. Existing private photo contributions are not made public.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

With your permission, Liftmaxing reads active and resting energy from Apple Health, including activity synced from Apple Watch, and writes dietary energy for newly confirmed food entries. Health readings are displayed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, used for advertising, or sold. Food syncing is optional. You can turn it off in the Food tab and manage individual permissions in Apple Health. Turning syncing off does not erase previously saved Health entries. Removing a synced food entry queues removal of its corresponding app-owned Health sample; failed removals remain pending until successfully retried.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information, use it for third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. We disclose information only to service providers needed to operate Liftmaxing, when you direct us to, when legally required, or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while your account exists. Settings provides data export and permanent account deletion. Deletion removes the Supabase Auth account and associated cloud profile, then clears local account data and the food diary. When available, stored Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked as part of deletion. Entries already saved to Apple Health remain there after account deletion; manage or delete them in the Health app.

Your choices

You may sign out, export your data, delete your account, or manage provider authorization from Apple or Google settings. Camera and Apple Health permissions can be managed in iOS settings. Food can be entered manually without camera or Health access. To request privacy help, use our support page.

Children

Liftmaxing is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account if you are below the minimum age required in your country.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
